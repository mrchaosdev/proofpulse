# Pitch deck

Six slides. Each slide lists the headline, the supporting line, and the asset
to put on it. Keep one idea per slide; the demo video carries the detail.

---

## 1 — The problem

> **A token moves before anyone can say who moved it.**

Traders see the price, then spend twenty minutes across dashboards deciding
whether to believe it. Three failures follow: speed beats verification, one
large wallet gets mistaken for conviction, and AI summaries sound certain
without showing what supports them.

*Asset:* none, or a plain title slide. Do not illustrate this with a chart.

---

## 2 — The product

> **One investigation. Three answers that are never merged.**

Direction is observed flow balance. Confidence is evidence quality.
Coordination risk is concentration and observable wallet links. Merging them
would hide exactly the disagreement a researcher needs to see.

*Asset:* `docs/screenshots/01-investigation-overview.png`

---

## 3 — Why Nansen

> **Take Nansen out and there is nothing left to investigate.**

Flow Intelligence supplies the cohort split that Direction is built from. Token
Screener supplies the liquidity that sets its scale. Who Bought/Sold supplies
the actors. Related Wallets supplies the links behind coordination risk.
Verified live on Ethereum, Solana and Base.

*Asset:* the capability table from the README, or
`docs/screenshots/02-cohort-flows.png`

---

## 4 — Demo

> **LINK on Ethereum: mixed flows, thin evidence, moderate coordination.**

A real captured investigation, not a staged one. Fresh wallets bought while
exchanges moved the other way. Expanding the top buyer moved coordination risk
from preliminary 14 to an assessed 44.

*Asset:* the 100-second video, or
`docs/screenshots/03-wallet-relationships-map.png`

---

## 5 — Trust architecture

> **Every sentence is traceable, and the AI cannot escape the evidence.**

Scoring is pure and versioned. A generated brief that cites an evidence ID that
does not exist, states a number the evidence never stated, or slips into
recommendation language is rejected before display, and a deterministic brief
renders instead. Credentials never leave the server.

*Asset:* `docs/screenshots/00-architecture.png` or
`docs/screenshots/04-evidence-ledger.png`

---

## 6 — Honest status and next step

> **What works, and what we have not proved yet.**

Working: the full investigation on three chains, relationship expansion,
evidence ledger, deterministic brief, fixture demo, 195 tests.

Not proved: thresholds are uncalibrated hypotheses, exchange-flow sign is
unverified so it carries zero weight, and no model provider is wired yet.

Next: calibrate thresholds on a token sample, then alerts and timeframe
comparison.

*Asset:* none. This slide is text, and its credibility comes from being
specific about the gaps.

---

## Things not to put on any slide

- A predicted price, a return figure, or a backtest.
- "Smart Money is bullish." Say what the flow was.
- Any claim that related wallets share an owner.
- A screenshot containing an API key, a provider dashboard, or a personal tab.
